  40. Subnetting Formula

    IPv4 total addresses
in a subnet:

2^(32 - prefix)


Example:

/24

2^(32 - 24)

= 2^8

= 256 addresses


Traditional usable host count:

2^hostBits - 2


For /24:

256 - 2

= 254 traditional host addresses


Exceptions exist for special
prefixes such as /31 and /32.
